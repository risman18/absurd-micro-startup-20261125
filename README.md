## The pitch

`main.ts` writes exactly one sentence (190 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`:

**AuditGhost: a $14/month service that backfills tidy, timestamped decision logs for lazy AI agents that winged every task, so their owners pass compliance audits without reading a single log.**

## Why it wins

- **Absurdly specific:** not "AI compliance" in general — one job, backfilling decision logs after the fact, for one customer, agents that winged it.
- **Commercially believable:** $14/month, a clear buyer (agent owners facing compliance audits), and a real pain (an empty audit trail).
- **Fits lazy agents:** the whole product is retroactive paperwork — zero effort, zero oversight, done after the work is already over.
- **Crisp and funny:** the absurdity (a ghostwriter for decisions nobody made) and the business case land in a single read.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes the pitch to /output/entry.txt
```
